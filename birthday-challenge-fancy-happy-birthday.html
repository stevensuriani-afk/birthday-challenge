HOW TO PLAY

Each puzzle starts at $5.00.

Type a letter on your keyboard or click a letter on the screen.

Each consonant you guess reduces the puzzle value by 10¢. Each vowel reduces it by 30¢.

You have 20 seconds between guesses and 4 minutes total for each puzzle.

Think you know it? Press SPACE to solve.

If you reveal every letter, the puzzle is solved automatically.

TEST MODE works exactly like the real game, but the money doesn't count. Practice as long as you want. When you're ready, leave Test Mode and play for real birthday money.

During the real game, press Ctrl + Shift + C if you want to cash out your Birthday Bank and end the game.

💵

CASH OUT?

Your Birthday Bank is $0.00.

If you cash out, the game ends and this becomes your final birthday winnings.

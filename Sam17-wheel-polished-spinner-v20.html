HOW TO PLAY

Each puzzle starts at $0.00. Build your winnings as you reveal letters.

This game is like Wheel of Fortune.

For a consonant, click SPIN. The values keep spinning until you press SPACE to stop them, or they stop automatically after 30 seconds. The spin can land on 25¢, 30¢, 50¢, 75¢, 💰 $1.00, 🎂 $2.00, or 😈 BANKRUPT. Then type or click one consonant. If it appears, you win the spun amount for each occurrence.

Five incorrect consonants ends the puzzle with $0 earned.

To guess a vowel, click BUY A VOWEL — 25¢. You must have at least 25¢ in the current puzzle. The 25¢ is charged when you buy the vowel, whether or not the vowel is in the puzzle.

You have 30 seconds between actions.

Think you know it? Click SOLVE. Once you do, you have 60 seconds to enter the full solution.

TEST MODE works exactly like the real game, but the money doesn't count. Practice as long as you want. When you're ready, leave Test Mode and play for real birthday money.

💵

CASH OUT?

Your Birthday Bank is $0.00.

If you cash out, the game ends and this becomes your final birthday winnings.

READY TO PLAY FOR REAL?

Once you choose to play for real, you can't go back to Test Mode.

The puzzles you play next will determine how much birthday money you win.

Once you start, you can play until you cash out. Other people can even play in your place if you want. But once you click CASH OUT, that's it.

Practice as much as you want before continuing.
